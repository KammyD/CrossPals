Alibaba.com and 1688.com are both run by Alibaba Group, and both sell to businesses. They are not, however, the same market. If you are building a private label, knowing which to use — and when — saves real money.

## The short version

**Alibaba.com** is built for overseas buyers: English interface, Trade Assurance, and suppliers who routinely export. **1688.com** is China's domestic wholesale market: lower prices, Chinese-only, and suppliers who usually don't ship abroad at all.

## Price

The same factory often lists the same product lower on 1688, because there is no export premium, no English support, and no international paperwork baked in. The gap is frequently 20–40% on identical goods. **Winner for price: 1688.**

## Language and access

1688 is Chinese-only. Search, chat, and negotiation all happen in Chinese, and many sellers won't reply to a foreign account. Alibaba is fully English. **Winner for access: Alibaba.**

## MOQs and flexibility

1688 sellers often accept much smaller quantities — sometimes a few dozen pieces — which suits testing a product before committing to a private label run. Alibaba MOQs are typically higher. **Winner for small tests: 1688.**

## Export and payment

Many 1688 suppliers can't export, can't quote international freight, and can't take an overseas payment. Alibaba suppliers are set up for all three. **Winner for end-to-end: Alibaba.**

## So which should you use?

Use **Alibaba** when you want a hand-holding, export-ready supplier and are willing to pay a little more for it. Use **1688** when you want the best price and small MOQs — and you have someone on the ground to buy, check, consolidate, and ship on your behalf.
