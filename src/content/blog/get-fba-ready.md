FBA prep is not glamorous. It is, however, the difference between a shipment that clears receiving in a day and one that is refused and shipped back at your expense. Here is the full checklist — in the order it should happen at the factory.

## 1. FNSKU labelling

Every sellable unit gets its own FNSKU label, printed to Amazon's size and clarity standards and applied flat to the packaging. If the unit has a barcode on the box already, Amazon requires that barcode to be covered. **Prep:** apply FNSKU, cover old barcodes, verify each one scans.

## 2. Poly-bagging

Anything loose, liquid, or prone to dust gets a poly-bag. Any bag with an opening over 5 inches needs a suffocation warning printed or applied on it. **Prep:** bag the unit, add the warning, seal it flat.

## 3. Bundling

Multi-packs and sets must be sealed so they arrive as one unit, labelled as a single SKU. **Prep:** shrink-wrap or bag the bundle, label the bundle, not the individual items.

## 4. Carton labels and packing list

Each carton carries a box label tied to your shipment plan. The packing list must match the physical contents, carton by carton. **Prep:** label every box, print a packing list that mirrors reality, hand it over with the shipment.

## 5. Weights and dimensions

Amazon has per-carton weight and size limits, and your shipment plan needs accurate measurements. Guessing causes surprises at receiving. **Prep:** weigh and measure every carton, record it, stay within limits.

## 6. The final photo check

Before the container closes: photos of labelled units, sealed bundles, labelled cartons, and the packing list. This is your proof if anything is questioned later. **Prep:** photograph everything, keep the record.
