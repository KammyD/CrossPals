An FBA shipment leaving China is a chain of small, unforgiving requirements. Miss one — a barcode that won't scan, a bag without a suffocation warning, a carton over the weight limit — and Amazon refuses the whole shipment. It goes back, or it sits. Either way, you pay.

Rejections are almost never about the product. They are about **prep**. Here is what actually causes them, and how to close each gap before your goods leave the factory.

## 1. Barcodes that don't scan

The single most common reason. FNSKU labels must be printed clearly, applied flat (never over a seam or corner), and scannable. A label printed too small, or applied over a curve, fails. **Check:** every unit carries its own correct FNSKU, and the barcode scans on the first pass.

## 2. Packaging that fails the bag rule

Any poly-bag with an opening over 5 inches needs a suffocation warning printed or labelled on it. Bags without it are rejected outright. **Check:** every bag has the warning, in the right size and language.

## 3. Bundles that come apart

Multi-packs must be sealed so they arrive as one unit. Loose items that were "meant to be together" arrive as separate SKUs — or not at all. **Check:** bundles are shrink-wrapped or bagged and labelled as a single unit.

## 4. Cartons outside the spec

Amazon has weight and dimension limits per carton. Oversized or over-weight boxes are refused. Carton labels must carry the right shipment and box IDs. **Check:** every carton measured, weighed, and labelled to your shipment plan.

## 5. Missing or wrong documentation

The packing list must match what is actually inside, box by box. A mismatch — even an honest one — triggers a manual review or refusal. **Check:** the packing list mirrors the physical cartons exactly.

## The fix: prep in China, not at the warehouse

Every one of these problems is cheap to fix at the factory and expensive to fix after the shipment reaches Amazon. That is why FBA prep belongs on the China side — labels applied, bags checked, bundles sealed, cartons weighed, packing list verified, all before the container closes.
